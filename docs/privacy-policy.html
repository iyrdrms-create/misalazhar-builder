[EN] Auto Form Builder ("Add-on") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Add-on interacts with your data when you use our services through Google Workspace.

[ID] Auto Form Builder ("Add-on") berkomitmen untuk melindungi privasi Anda. Kebijakan Privasi ini menjelaskan bagaimana Add-on kami berinteraksi dengan data Anda saat Anda menggunakan layanan kami melalui Google Workspace.

[EN] This Add-on requires specific permissions to function correctly:
